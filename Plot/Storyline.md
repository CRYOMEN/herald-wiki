# Overarching Storyline

The story of *Herald* is a dual-narrative epic that bridges the gap between the myths of Ancient Egypt and a technologically advanced future.

## Act I: The Awakening (The Prologue)
*   **Setting:** A hidden desert settlement in Egypt, 2070.
*   **Key Event:** The Secret Organization discovers the tomb of the Herald, Alekandras.
*   **Conflict:** The Followers of Cleopatra fight a losing battle to protect their "god." To save the mission, they attempt to wake Alekandras early.
*   **The Twist:** A malfunction during the battle causes a catastrophic energy drain, freezing the region and teleporting Alekandras to a modern metropolis.

## Act II: Shadows of the Metropolis
*   **Setting:** A sprawling megacity, 2070.
*   **The Protagonist's Journey:** Alekandras wakes in the sewers, weakened and alone. He must navigate a world that no longer worships him, find new allies (like Jasmine, a descendant of his ancient love, Iras), and avoid the Secret Organization's hunt.
*   **Themes:** Alienation, discovery, and the contrast between the "God of Egypt" and the "Beast of the Sewers."

## Act III: The Resonance
*   **Conflict:** The Secret Organization’s true motives are revealed—they are not just hunting technology but are inadvertently (or deliberately) preparing for the arrival of the Cosmic Threat.
*   **Development:** Alekandras must recover his suit's special energy, possibly through rediscovering human connection, and unite the hidden hybrid societies with modern humanity.

## Act IV: The Herald's Message
*   **Climax:** The Cosmic Threat arrives. The "Sister Planet" resonance is activated.
*   **Resolution:** humanity and the Heralds must combine silicon hardware with alien wetware to build a defense that can save both Earth and the remnants of the Homeworld.

## Key Story Questions
1.  **Why was Alekandras 130 years early?** Was it truly an accident, or did the suit's AI (Iko) perceive a threat that required an earlier awakening?
2.  **What is the "Cosmic Threat"?** Is it a biological predator, a sentient plague, or something even more fundamental to the universe?
3.  **The Legacy of Love:** How does the DNA modification performed by Iras affect Jasmine and the future of the mission?
