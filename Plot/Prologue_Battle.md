# Prologue: The Battle of the Settlement

## Scene Overview
The story opens with a high-stakes confrontation in the Egyptian desert. This scene serves as both a narrative hook and a recontextualization of history, introducing the main factions and the fundamental rules of the *Herald* universe.

## The Setting
A hidden desert settlement, home to the **Followers of Cleopatra**. For over 2,000 years, this community has guarded the secret tomb of the Herald **Alekandras** (known to them as the god Amun-Ra). In the year 2070, this ancient secret is finally discovered.

## The Participants

### 1. The Defenders: Followers of Cleopatra
*   **Leader:** The Settlement Leader (The primary POV for this scene).
*   **Forces:** A small militia armed with a mix of aging hardware and a few hybrid "monsters."
*   **Objective:** Protect the tomb at all costs, honoring the oath given to Queen Cleopatra VII.

### 2. The Invaders: The Secret Organization
*   **Commander:** A ruthless field officer (Organization Field Commander).
*   **Forces:** A corporate paramilitary force equipped with tanks, high-tech infantry, and combat drones.
*   **Objective:** Extract the Herald to seize his "wetware" technology.

## Sequence of Events

### 1. The Warning
**Iko** (the Herald’s Assistant) is scouting the desert perimeter when she detects a massive signal of approaching armor. She rushes back to the settlement to sound the alarm. The Leader, shocked that their sensors were bypassed, mobilizes the militia.

### 2. The Standoff
The Organization surrounds the settlement. The Commander issues a cold ultimatum: "Surrender the artifact, and you won't be harmed." The Leader, citing an oath older than the Organization's country, refuses.

### 3. The Siege
A heavy firefight ensues. Organization tanks shell the settlement’s defensive turrets while infantry squads begin a coordinated breach. The Followers fight bravely but are quickly overwhelmed by the Organization's superior silicon-based technology.

### 4. The Last Resort
Realizing the mission is failing, the Leader orders Iko to enter the tomb and initiate an emergency awakening procedure for Alekandras.

### 5. The Malfunction
As Iko begins the process, an Organization projectile hits the tomb's superstructure. The ancient life-support systems, already fragile after 2,000 years, suffer a catastrophic failure.

### 6. The Endothermic Surge
The damaged Living Suit enters an uncontrolled "emergency harvest" mode to jump-start the Herald's vitals. It begins aggressively draining thermal energy from the environment.
- **The Deep Freeze:** The temperature drops from 40°C to **-200°C (near absolute zero)** in a matter of minutes.
- **Devastation:** Attacking soldiers and defending Followers alike are frozen instantly. The Organization Commander survives only by retreating into his vehicle's reinforced thermal shielding.
- **The Vortex:** A massive hurricane vortex, 15km in radius, forms over the desert as a result of the extreme temperature imbalance.

### 7. The Teleportation Anomaly
At the peak of the energy surge, the instability punches a hole in local spacetime. A teleportation anomaly occurs, and the Herald's capsule is ripped from the desert, vanishing into a localized rift.

## Aftermath & Transition
The scene ends on a shot of the silent, frozen wasteland. Twelve days later, the capsule reappears in the center of a modern metropolis. A massive sonic boom shatters windows for miles as the pod crashes into the asphalt, breaking through into the city's sewer system.

To protect the weakened Herald, Iko initiates a **biomass decomposition** protocol, causing the capsule to melt into organic sludge and drain into the sewers, leaving only a hollow metallic frame for the authorities to find.

## Visual Cues
- **Thermal Contrast:** The blistering yellow of the desert vs. the sudden, crystalline blue of the frost.
- **The Vortex:** A dark, swirling hurricane lit by flashes of bio-electric lightning.
- **Technology Clash:** The clinical, gray hardware of the Organization against the organic, pulsing "wetware" of the Herald's tomb.
