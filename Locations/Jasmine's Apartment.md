# Jasmine's Apartment

## Overview

The high-rise residence of [Jasmine Foster](../Characters/Jasmine Foster.md) in the [Metropolis](Metropolis.md). Jasmine moves here after her [outskirts home](Jasmine's Home.md) is affected by the Herald's capsule crash.

## Description

- **Type:** High-rise residential apartment
- **Location:** Top floor of a skyscraper in the metropolis
- **Era:** 2070 AD

## Interior

### Bedroom

The bedroom reflects Jasmine's personality — cozy yet vibrant:

- **Walls:** Transparent, reflective glass providing a stunning view of the city skyline. Bright, colorful interior wall surfaces
- **Floors:** Smooth, polished material covered with soft, plush carpets
- **Furniture:** A mix of antique and modern pieces; a comfortable queen-sized bed as the centerpiece; a full-length mirror
- **Decor:** Seashells, crystals, dried flowers, and other personal trinkets and mementos

### Architectural Features

- **Sliding walls:** Sections of the walls can open, exposing the room directly to the outside air at high altitude
- **Transparent roof:** The ceiling is see-through, allowing a direct view of the sky above

### Technology

- **Wall console:** A small console built into the wall with a user interface for interaction
- **Neural interface:** Located near the console; Jasmine uses it to access the virtual world and connect with friends

## Story Significance

- **Strategic Location:** Provides a high vantage point for Jasmine to monitor the Herald's crash site in the city.
- **Atmospheric Encounters:** [Olf](../Characters/Olf.md) (Wolf) visits Jasmine here at night, climbing the skyscraper to enter through the window.
