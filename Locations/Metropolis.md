# The Metropolis

## Overview

The primary urban setting of the 2070 story. A vast, futuristic city where humanity's technological advances are on full display — towering architecture, ubiquitous AI, and a hidden underworld beneath its streets.

## Description

- **Era:** 2070 AD
- **Type:** Megacity / futuristic metropolis
- **Scale:** Massive — skyscrapers, some reaching up to 2.5 km in height

## Cityscape

The metropolis is defined by its towering skyscrapers and sleek flying vehicles that hum through the air at all hours. Holographic advertisements flicker across the sky, casting shifting light over the streets below. AI-syns (artificial synthetic beings) walk the streets alongside humans, and all kinds of advanced machines move about the city.

The city never truly sleeps. At night, lights flash across buildings, flying cars zoom past, and the streets bustle with people, AI-syns, and exotic machinery. By early morning, the skyline glows with a mix of artificial and natural light.

## Technology

- **Flying vehicles:** Sleek cars that travel through the air, a primary mode of transportation
- **Holographic advertisements:** Projected into the sky above the city
- **AI-syns:** Artificial synthetic beings integrated into daily city life
- **Neuroreality:** Neural interfaces used by residents to access virtual worlds and connect with others
- **Wall consoles:** User interfaces built into residential spaces
- **Robotics and prosthetics:** Advanced systems ubiquitous throughout society
- **Alternative energy:** Powers the city's vast infrastructure

## Government

The metropolis operates under an partial AI-controlled government. The government is determined to maintain control and will act aggressively against perceived threats — including the Herald after his capsule crashes into the city.

## Underground

Beneath the metropolis lies an extensive sewer and tunnel system that serves as the hidden habitat of the [monster community](../Factions/Monsters.md). This underground world is far more vast and complex than the surface dwellers realize.
