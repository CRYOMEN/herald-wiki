# Sewers

## Overview

An extensive sewer and tunnel system running beneath the metropolis. Far more than just drainage infrastructure — the sewers are central to the underground community's way of life.

## Description

- **Type:** Underground tunnel network
- **Location:** Beneath the metropolis (2070 AD)
- **Scale:** Vast — extends far beyond the city limits. Remote tunnels are largely unexplored.
- **Environment:** Damp, dark, with flowing water channels, walkways along main channels, waterfalls in certain areas, alcoves behind water curtains.

## Inhabitants

Home to the [underground monster community](../Factions/Monsters.md) and various local animals hunted for meat by residents.

## Significance

- Where [Jasmine's Kidnapping](../Events/Jasmine's Kidnapping.md) takes place — her first encounter with the underground world
- Site of Alekandras' capsule disposal after the [Battle Scene](../Events/Battle Scene.md)
- The sewers connect to the broader underground cities that exist all around the world

## Connected locations

- [Underground Tavern](Underground Tavern.md) — a gathering place within the tunnel network
- [Herald's Lair](Herald's Lair.md) — Alekandras' biological hideout deep in the tunnels
