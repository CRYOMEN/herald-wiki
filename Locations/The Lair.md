# The Lair

## Overview

The underground hideout of [Alekandras](../Characters/Alekandras.md) (one of the herald), located deep within the [sewer system](Sewers.md) beneath the [Metropolis](Metropolis.md).

## Description

- **Type:** Hidden lair / living quarters
- **Location:** Deep underground, within the [sewers](Sewers.md) beneath the metropolis
- **Nature:** Biological — built using alien biological technology, not human materials. The lair is organic, grown rather than constructed.

## Characteristics

- **Biological construction:** The lair is made of living, organic material — a product of [Herald/alien technology](../Technology/Alien Tech.md). It regenerates and sustains itself.
- **Regenerative:** Damage to the lair's structure heals over time.
- **No riches or luxury:** The lair is functional and austere. It is a place of survival and purpose, not comfort.

## Story Significance

- Where [Alekandras](../Characters/Alekandras.md) hides after crashing into the metropolis
- A location [Jasmine Foster](../Characters/Jasmine Foster.md) visits as part of her underground journey
