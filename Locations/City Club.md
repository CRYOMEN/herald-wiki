# City Club

## Overview

A nightclub in the metropolis where [Jasmine Foster](../Characters/Jasmine.md) moonlights as a dancer under the stage name "Scarlett."

## Description

- **Type:** Nightclub / entertainment venue
- **Location:** Metropolis
- **Clientele:** Male and female clientele seeking exclusive, secretive entertainment
- **Atmosphere:** Clandestine, dimly lit, mysterious. Performers wear masks to maintain anonymity.

## Significance

- The venue where [Jasmine Foster](../Characters/Jasmine.md) performs under her alter ego — see her character sheet for details on her double life and dance style

## Connected characters

- [Jasmine Foster](../Characters/Jasmine.md) — dancer (as Scarlett)
