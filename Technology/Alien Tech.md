# Alien Technology

## Overview

Alien technology is tied to bioengineering - pure "wetware." Aliens are experts in how the body works, how cells function, and how to build and develop living bodies. Where human tech is rigid and dry (silicon, metal, plastic, wires pushing electricity to simulate thinking), Alien technology is alive: living organisms, biomass, symbiotic systems.

See [Human Technology](Human Tech.md) for a comparison with human technology.


## 1. Living Suit (Exoskeleton)

### 1.1 Nature and appearance

- The Living Suit is a **living organism** - a **symbiotic exoskeleton** that is alive and functions as an additional body. Think of it less like an Iron Man suit and more like a massive genetically engineered creature that you ride inside of. The alien sits inside it; what people see is the suit's distinctive outward form.
- **Origin and development:** The Living Suit represents the **pinnacle of bioengineering** from the alien homeworld. It was constructed in haste as part of an emergency response to the cosmic threat — not all of its functions and capabilities were fully understood or documented by its creators. Some mechanisms were discovered only through usage. The suit has the appearance of a **dragon** — a massive, imposing creature with armored skin, multiple limbs, and a prominent head with massive jaw.
- **Connection interface:** The occupant controls the suit through a **neural interface** - they literally share a nervous system. The owner and suit function as **one**-direct sensorimotor feedback, not a "cockpit" experience. (Injury, exhaustion, or overload can propagate across the link.)
- **Typical form:** Imposing, non-humanoid - powerful build, roughly 2.4 meters tall, multiple limbs (often four, sometimes two), prominent head and massive jaw, thick armored skin. Morphology varies by herald.
- **Variation between suits:** Different heralds have suits that differ in shape and look. Some variants have flight capability; others use different technology to move or fly.
- **Self-modification:** Suits can self-modify within certain limits. For example, the occupant can change the suit's configuration-e.g. reduce appendages and move on four limbs only.

### 1.2 Power and maintenance

- **Energy source:** The exoskeleton eats - it has a mouth and a full digestive system. It requires caloric intake, consuming organic matter (plants, animals, whatever is available). The suit uses this to heal and repair itself. The food is converted into energy, building blocks for cells, and waste, going from being ground in the mouth to being broken down in the stomach and absorbing nutrients (glucose, amino acids, and fatty acids) into the bloodstream through the small intestine. Undigested waste is converted into feces in the large intestine and eliminated from the body. The entire process takes 2 to 5 days.
- **Special energy:** A finite, difficult-to-recharge energy source essential for the alien occupant's survival and for powering advanced capabilities (flight, life-form creation). Its depletion is the primary reason the protagonist entered hibernation.
- **Recharging via Neurochemical Resonance:** Discovered accidentally by Alekandras, the suit can harvest a small amount of "special energy" from the bioelectric output of intense physical and emotional intimacy. This hidden function converts profound emotional connections into a survival resource.

### 1.3 Capabilities

- **Life-form creation:** The suit is a mobile laboratory - a genetic synthesizer. It can sample DNA from the local environment and create new viable life forms. It can replicate a creature it has observed, or combine DNA (e.g. dog + cow + other elements, or adding wings) to create hybrids. It functions as a mobile laboratory for mixing DNA and creating new species. Creating these beings consumes the suit's special energy (see above).

### 1.4 Biomass decomposition

- Artificially created life forms (e.g. monsters) decompose quickly after death, leaving biomass - they revert back to their base proteins. This is why no skeletons are ever found. It is a self-cleaning mechanism built into the genetic constructs.

### 1.5 Suit assistant

- The suit does not operate alone; it has an **assistant**. This is a standard feature of all alien suits. See [Iko](../Characters/Iko.md) character sheet for example.
- **Nature:** The assistant is alive and sentient - it can think, has fears, worries, emotions, possesses and can acquire knowledge, and can speak. Functions as a biological drone.
- **Role:** Takes measurements of the suit; can **detach** and act as a separate unit. Teaches the alien; knows a lot about technology.
- **Loyalty:** Always 100% loyal to whoever is currently inside the suit.

## 2. Settlement Concealment Technology

### 2.1 Nature and Origin

- **Classification:** Alien technology (not human-made)
- **Activation date:** Approximately 2,000 years ago (~30 BCE) in Ancient Egypt
- **Historical context:** Activated when humanity possessed no comparable technological capabilities
- **Technological basis:** Based on the same bioengineering principles as the Living Suit and other alien systems, though serving a fundamentally different purpose

### 2.2 Purpose and Function

The concealment technology was deployed to hide [the settlement](../Locations/Settlement.md) of Cleopatra's followers who guarded the sleeping Herald. Unlike the Living Suit which focuses on biological enhancement and life-form creation, this system manipulates spatial perception on a macro scale.

**Technical designation:** "Zero-Point Reality Intersection"

**Core mechanism:**
- Creates a primitive rift in space—a zero-point intersection of realities
- Causes the settlement and everything within its perimeter to exist in a slightly shifted dimensional state
- External observers perceive only empty desert when physically present at the settlement's location
- All conventional detection methods (satellite imagery, aerial reconnaissance, ground sensors) fail to register the settlement's presence

### 2.3 Operational Properties

**For external observers:**
- Physical access to the geographic area is possible, but the settlement remains invisible and intangible
- No buildings, people, or infrastructure can be detected by any known human technology (pre-2070)

**For residents:**
- Free passage in and out of the concealment field by crossing the perimeter boundary
- Outward crossing: instant transition into normal reality, becoming visible to outside observers
- Inward crossing: "vanishing" from external perspective, entering the concealed space
- Transitions are seamless and imperceptible to residents themselves

**Spatial characteristics:**
- Covers the entire settlement area with vertical extension
- Does not affect physical space itself—only affects perception and detection from outside
- Physical matter remains fully tangible within the field

## 3. Memory-Wipe Technology

### 3.1 Nature and Purpose

Memory-wipe technology is an alien bioengineering system capable of selectively removing, implanting, or altering memories in a sentient being. It can also modify personality and identity to varying degrees.

**Primary use (forced, without consent):**
- Outsiders who accidentally or intentionally discover the [Hidden Monster Community](../Factions/Monsters.md) have their memories of the encounter wiped and are released
- Community members who are expelled may have their memories modified as part of the expulsion

**Secondary use (accepted by subject):**
- Community members who choose to leave permanently undergo memory modification to allow safe reintegration into mainstream society — they accept the procedure as a condition of departure, but do not control it

**Origin:**
- Based on the same alien bioengineering principles as the Living Suit and other technologies
- Represents advanced understanding of neurological structures and memory formation

### 3.2 Technical Mechanism

**Process:**
- Targets specific neural pathways associated with memories of events
- Disrupts and dissolves the synaptic connections that encode these specific memories

**Selectivity:**
- Can selectively remove memories related to specific subjects
- Does not affect general knowledge or skills acquired during the time period

**Administration:**
- Requires trained individuals within the community to operate
- Process details (TBD)

### 3.3 Capabilities 

- Can remove specific memories selectively
- Can implant false memories (events that never happened)
- Can alter personality and identity to varying degrees
- May leave gaps in personal timeline that the subject rationalizes unconsciously

**For information about how this technology was used in the settlement context, see [Settlement](../Locations/Settlement.md).**
