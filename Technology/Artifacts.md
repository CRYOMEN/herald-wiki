## 2. Memory Unlock Artifact (created by Iko)

**Status:** Active in story

**Creator:** [Iko](../Characters/Iko.md)

**History:** A family heirloom passed down through the [Foster family](../Characters/Jasmine Foster.md) women ([Eleanor](../Characters/Eleanor Foster.md), [Grace](../Characters/Grace Foster.md)) for generations. The family perceives it as a simple, though precious, "trinket" or memento, unaware of its true nature.

**Purpose:** Opens/unlocks hidden internal memory encoded within [Jasmine Foster](../Characters/Jasmine Foster.md)'s DNA. This artifact reveals information about Jasmine's true nature or past that is stored at a genetic/biological level (specifically memories of her ancestor [Iras](../Characters/Iras.md)).

**Significance:** Provides access to dormant knowledge or abilities within [Jasmine Foster](../Characters/Jasmine Foster.md) that are crucial to the narrative.

**Related:**
- [Jasmine Foster](../Characters/Jasmine Foster.md) (subject — has encoded memory in DNA)
- [Iko](../Characters/Iko.md) (creator)
- [Eleanor Foster](../Characters/Eleanor Foster.md) (who passed it to Jasmine)
