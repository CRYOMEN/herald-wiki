# Herald Project Wiki

**Wiki & Design Documentation** for the game and story project *Herald*.

Welcome to the official repository for *Herald*, a science fiction epic that bridges the gap between Ancient Egyptian mythology and a high-tech future. This wiki serves as a comprehensive "Codex" for developers, writers, and fans, detailing the biology, technology, and history of the Heralds and their mission on Earth.

## 📂 Project Structure

The wiki is organized into the following departments:

- [**Lore/**](Lore/README.md): Foundational history, the Homeworld, the Resonance theory, and major factions.
- [**Characters/**](Characters/README.md): Detailed profiles of protagonists, antagonists, and the comprehensive **Bestiary** of hybrid species.
- [**Tech/**](Tech/README.md): A comparative look at Alien Biotechnology ("Wetware") and human Silicon Technology ("Hardware").
- [**Plot/**](Plot/README.md): Narrative arcs, storylines, and detailed scene breakdowns (including the Prologue).
- [**Game/**](Game/README.md): Master design documents, gameplay mechanics, and technical specifications.

## 🌍 The Premise

An alien Herald, once worshiped as the god Amun-Ra in Ancient Egypt, awakens in the year 2070. He finds a world that has forgotten his original message—a warning of an encroaching cosmic threat that destroyed his own binary planet system. Now, hiding in the shadows of a modern metropolis, he must find allies among the descendants of his past and unite humanity before the extinction-level event reaches Earth.
