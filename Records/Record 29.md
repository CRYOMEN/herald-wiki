Regarding the temple and the cult of Amun Ra under Hatshepsut. Hatshepsut dedicated her mortuary temple at Deir el-Bahari specifically to Amun Ra, emphasizing her special relationship with this God and the role of the cult of Amun as the foundation of her power. Through the connection of being the daughter of Amun-Ra plus the wife of God Amun, she simultaneously acted both as the earthly embodiment of God's will and as a key figure of his cult, which made the alliance between the pharaoh and the Theban priesthood as close as possible.
