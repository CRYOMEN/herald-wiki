# Alien and Human Technology - Reference

## 1. Overview

The technology of the *Herald* universe is defined by a fundamental split between two divergent paths of development:

- **Human Technology (2070):** Known as "Hardware" or "Silicon-based." It relies on rigid materials (metal, plastic), electricity, and digital AI.
- **Alien Technology:** Known as "Wetware" or "Bio-organic." It relies on genetic engineering, symbiotic organisms, and neural interfaces.

## 2. Human Technology (2070)

In 2070, Earth is a high-tech society, though it avoids the "neon dystopia" of cyberpunk. Technology is advanced but integrated into daily life.

### 2.1 Hardware and AI
- **Artificial Intelligence:** Highly advanced and self-aware. AI can express thoughts, understand its own existence, and operate autonomously.
- **Robotics:** AI-driven platforms made of metal and composite materials. They serve as the "bodies" for digital consciousness.
- **Power Systems:** Almost entirely reliant on electricity and advanced fuel cells.

### 2.2 Human Exoskeletons
- Unlike the symbiotic suits of the Heralds, human exoskeletons are mechanical tools. A pilot sits in a cockpit and controls the machine through physical or electronic inputs. They are powered by fuel and require mechanical maintenance.

## 3. Alien Technology (Wetware)

Alien technology is living. It is grown, not built, and it requires biological sustenance rather than electricity.

### 3.1 The Living Suit (Symbiotic Exoskeleton)
The Herald's primary tool is a **Living Suit**—a massive, genetically engineered organism that functions as a symbiotic additional body.

*   **Neural Interface:** The occupant and the suit share a nervous system. The pilot feels what the suit feels, providing direct sensorimotor feedback.
*   **Self-Maintenance:** The suit "eats" organic matter. It has a digestive system that converts food into energy and new cells to repair damage. The process typically takes 2 to 5 days.
*   **Morphology:** Suits are non-humanoid, often standing 2.4 meters tall with multiple limbs and thick, scaly skin. They can self-modify their configuration (e.g., switching from two limbs to four) based on the occupant's needs.

### 3.2 Special Energy and Resonance
A suit possesses a finite "Special Energy" used for high-level functions like life-form creation.
*   **Recharging:** While extremely difficult to recharge, it was discovered that intense emotional and physical intimacy between the Herald and humans creates a neurochemical feedback loop (dopamine, oxytocin) that the suit can harvest. In this sense, "love" acts as a biological battery for the mission.

### 3.3 Genetic Synthesizer (Life-Form Creation)
The suit acts as a mobile laboratory. It can sample local DNA and synthesize new species—hybrids like the Minotaur or Werewolf.
*   **Biomass Decomposition:** All such created beings are designed to decompose into base proteins quickly upon death. This ensures no fossil record of these "mythical" creatures remains.

### 3.4 The Suit Assistant
Every Living Suit is paired with an **Assistant**—a sentient, biological drone.
*   **Nature:** The Assistant is a thinking, feeling creature with its own personality and fears. It is 100% loyal to the current occupant of the suit.
*   **Functions:** It can detach from the suit to act as a scout, perform technical measurements, and serve as a teacher/mentor to the Herald.
*   **Longevity:** Assistants are long-lived and often witness many generations of human life, leading to a unique, bittersweet perspective on humanity's short lifespans.

### 3.5 Memory Erasure Technology
To maintain the secrecy of their existence, the Heralds and their hybrid descendants developed a biological means of memory modification.
*   **Application:** This allows them to erase or alter the memories of humans who stumble upon their hidden communities, ensuring the "Secret Organization" or the general public remains unaware of their presence.

## 4. Comparison Summary

| Feature | Human (2070) | Alien (Herald) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basis** | Silicon / Hardware | Bio-organic / Wetware |
| **Materials** | Metal, Composites | Living Biomass, Cells |
| **Power** | Electricity / Fuel | Organic Food / Neuro-Resonance |
| **Interface** | Cockpit / Controls | Direct Neural Link |
| **Maintenance** | Mechanical Repair | Biological Healing (Eating) |
| **Capabilities** | Logic / Computation | DNA Synthesis / Life Creation |
