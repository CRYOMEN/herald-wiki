# Game Design Document: Herald – The Awakening

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Title:** Herald: The Awakening
- **Logline:** An alien Herald, once worshiped as a god in Ancient Egypt, wakes in the year 2070 to find a world that has forgotten his message. To save both his dying Homeworld and a modern Earth, he must navigate a landscape of corporate greed and ancient secrets to find the one woman who holds the key to his mission.
- **Premise:** The story centers on the "Unified Theory of Mythology," revealing that the gods and monsters of ancient cultures were actually advanced biological entities. The game explores themes of betrayal, love, and the clash between "wetware" (alien biotechnology) and "hardware" (human silicon technology).
- **Core Hook:** Recontextualizing all of human history as a "Planetary Cargo Cult"—humanity spent thousands of years worshiping the messenger while completely missing the message.

## 2. Project Scope

- **Genre:** 3DCG Narrative Adventure / RPG Sandbox.
- **Tone:** Epic, Intimate, Hopeful, and Mature.
- **Format:** High-end Video Game / Episodic Novel.
- **Target Audience:** Mature (Adult) audience, focusing on deep world-building and narrative choice.

## 3. World & Setting

- **Timeline:** Dual-narrative focus on **Ancient Egypt** (flashbacks) and **2070 AD** (main timeline).
- **Key Locations:**
    - The Egyptian Desert (The Settlement).
    - The Modern Metropolis (The Surface and the Sewers).
    - The Homeworld (Flashbacks/Lore).
- **Factions:**
    - **Followers of Cleopatra:** The ancient guardians of the Herald.
    - **The Secret Organization:** A high-tech paramilitary corporation seeking to weaponize alien biotech.
    - **The Hybrid Society:** Secretive descendants of the Heralds' ancient creations (Bestiary).

## 4. Characters

- **The Herald (Alekandras):** An impulsive, idealistic alien survivor hiding in the modern world.
- **Jasmine:** A modern dancer and student who carries the genetic memories of the Herald's ancient love.
- **Iko:** The Herald's sentient biological assistant and mentor.
- **The Commander:** A ruthless corporate antagonist seeking to recover "high-value assets."

## 5. Gameplay Mechanics

- **Core Loop:** Exploration → Narrative Interaction (Dialogue/Choice) → Relationship Development → Progression toward Multiple Endings.
- **The Resonance System:** Relationship building with Jasmine and other allies serves as a "battery" to recharge the Herald's Special Energy.
- **Genetic Synthesis:** Gameplay moments involving the creation or direction of hybrid creatures (Bestiary).
- **Choices & Consequences:** Decisions made in the past (flashbacks) and present (2070) ripple through the narrative, affecting the final defense against the Cosmic Threat.

## 6. Aesthetics & Tone

- **Visual Style:** 3DCG, semi-realistic. Contrast between the organic, "wet" look of alien tech and the sharp, clinical "hardware" of 2070.
- **Inspiration:** *Stargate*, *Mass Effect*, *Avatar*, *Crysis*.
