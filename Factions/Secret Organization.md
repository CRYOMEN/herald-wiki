# Faction: Secret Organization

## Overview

A powerful paramilitary corporation of unknown origin. Founded by power-hungry humans who encountered alien technology and wanted it for themselves, the organization has spent an unknown but considerable span of time hunting aliens, their artifacts, and their creations. By 2070 it has evolved into a high-tech, incredibly wealthy corporation with military-grade capabilities far exceeding what public knowledge would suggest.

## History

### Foundation (Unknown)

The organization's origins are unknown — when it was founded, by whom exactly, and under what circumstances remains unclear. What is known is that at some point in history, a group of humans encountered alien technology (the Living Suit, bio-engineered creatures, concealment systems) and chose to pursue it for their own power rather than serve the Herald's mission. How long ago this occurred, and whether the organization existed in a recognizable form from that moment or evolved gradually, is TBD.

### Evolution (Unknown → 2070)

Over an unknown but considerable span of time, the organization grew from what may have been a secretive cult or cabal into a fully modern corporation. They hunted both heralds and their creations — collecting alien artifacts, studying alien biotechnology, and gradually accumulating resources and intelligence.

### 2070

By the present day the organization possesses:
- Heavy military hardware (tanks, infantry, artillery)
- Advanced intelligence capabilities
- Possibly reverse-engineered alien technology (see [Settlement — How the Secret Organization Discovered It](../Locations/Settlement.md))

## Goals

- Obtain alien technology — the Living Suit and all related systems
- Eliminate or capture the Herald ([Alekandras](../Characters/Alekandras.md)) and other aliens
- Expand their technological and military superiority

## Connection to Apophis (Theory)

There is speculation that [Apophis](../Characters/Apophis.md) — a rogue herald driven into hiding after his war with Alekandras — may be connected to the foundation or evolution of the Secret Organization. If true, the organization may be unwitting pawns or active collaborators with a hostile alien. TBD.

## Key Events

- **[The Awakening Battle](../Events/Battle Scene.md)** — The organization's assault on the settlement in 2070. They arrived with tanks and infantry, breached the concealment field, and triggered a catastrophic malfunction that froze the entire area. Their field commander survived by sheltering inside military equipment.
