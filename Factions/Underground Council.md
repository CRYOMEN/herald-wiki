# Underground Council

## Overview

The governing body of the [hidden monster community](Monsters.md) beneath the [Metropolis](../Locations/Metropolis.md). Enforces the laws and secrecy that keep the underground world hidden from surface society.

## Structure

- **Type:** Governing council
- **Jurisdiction:** The entire underground community — [sewers](../Locations/Sewers.md) and subterranean settlement
- **Authority:** Sets and enforces laws for all underground inhabitants

## Laws and Enforcement

- **Secrecy above all:** The underground world must remain hidden from surface-dwellers. Bringing a surface human underground is strictly forbidden.
- **Memory-wipe hunts:** When an outsider discovers the underground, the Council dispatches hunters to find and mind-wipe them — erasing all knowledge of the underground from the intruder's memory.
- **Consequences for violations:** Those who break the secrecy rules face severe punishment from the Council.

## Story Significance

- [Hassan](../Characters/Hassan.md) violated the Council's law by kidnapping [Jasmine Foster](../Characters/Jasmine.md) — a surface human — and bringing her underground.
- The Council's secrecy enforcement creates constant tension between the underground and surface worlds.
