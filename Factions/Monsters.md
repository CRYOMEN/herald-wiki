# The Hidden Monster Community

## Overview

The Hidden Monster Community is a secret global society of monsters — descendants of bio-engineered creatures originally created by Heralds at various points in the past (some current members may also be directly Herald-created). This community has existed in parallel with human civilization for several thousand years, largely unknown to mainstream humanity, and includes humans who are aware of and integrated into this hidden society. See [Bestiary](../Characters/Bestiary.md) for more details.

**Key facts:**
- **Formation period:** Began at various points in history when different Heralds visited Earth; coalesced into a unified community by 2070 CE
- **Geographic scope:** Global, with population centers in various locations
- **Visibility:** Almost completely unknown to mainstream humanity
- **Composition:** Monsters from various Heralds + humans who know about and live among them (including some with criminal backgrounds)
- **Estimated population:** Very small relative to global human population, distributed across the planet

## Laws and Governance

### Secrecy Laws

The community's most important laws revolve around maintaining absolute secrecy from mainstream humanity.

**Law: Exposure Prevention**
- Community members must not willingly expose the community's existence to unaware humans
- Violations are treated as serious offenses

**Law: Memory Modification**
- If a human accidentally or intentionally discovers the community, their memory of the encounter is wiped and they are released — no harm, no further action. See [Technology](../Technology/AlienTech.md) for technical details
- Community members (human only) who wish to leave permanently may also have their memories modified to allow safe reintegration into mainstream society

### Criminal Law

The community has laws governing criminal behavior, though with different tolerances than human society.

**Tolerance levels:**
- **High tolerance:** Minor theft, fraud, slavery, escape from human justice for non-violent crimes
- **Moderate tolerance:** Property crimes, non-lethal violence in self-defense
- **Zero tolerance:** Murder, extreme violence, crimes that risk exposing the community

**Integration of human criminals:**
- Monsters have a somewhat higher tolerance for criminal behavior than mainstream human society
- Partially due to monsters' nature (DNA from animals, some "wildness" in behavior)
- Humans fleeing human law may find refuge in the community if their crimes fall within acceptable limits
- Careful vetting process determines whether a fugitive human can be trusted

## Secrecy and Survival Strategies

### Why the Community Remains Hidden

**Primary reasons:**

1. **Fear of government exploitation**
   - Governments would want to capture and study monsters to advance human technology
   - Risk of being sent to government laboratories for experimentation
   - Monsters' biology represents valuable scientific and military assets

2. **Power imbalance**
   - Monster population is very small compared to billions of humans
   - Humans in power seek control, wealth, and dominance
   - Monsters could easily be subjugated and exploited due to their small numbers

3. **Increasing human technological capability**
   - In early history, humans lacked the technology to effectively capture or study monsters
   - By the 20th-21st centuries, human technology advanced rapidly
   - Modern surveillance, weapons, and scientific capabilities make hiding increasingly necessary

4. **Historical self-preservation**
   - From the beginning, monsters understood humans' tendency toward fear and violence against the "other"
   - Remaining hidden was always the safest strategy
   - As technology improved, the stakes became even higher

### How Secrecy is Maintained

**Urban concealment:**
- Underground infrastructure (sewers, tunnels, abandoned spaces) provides hiding places
- Part of the monster community lives beneath major cities
- Nocturnal activity patterns to avoid human contact
- Careful management of evidence (biological waste, tracks, sounds)
- Dead monsters leave no physical evidence (no skeletons to discover)
- Possible use of smaller-scale concealment technology or techniques (TBD)

**Social concealment:**
- Careful vetting of new human members
- Strict protocols about contact with mainstream humanity
- Monsters may use disguises or shapeshifting if capable (TBD)
- Humans tend to rationalize away things that don't fit their worldview, reducing risk of exposure
