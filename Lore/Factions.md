# Factions

The world of 2070 is shaped by the legacy of the Heralds and the clandestine groups that have formed around their technology.

## 1. The Followers of Cleopatra
A secretive society that has guarded the Herald for over 2,000 years.

*   **Origins:** Founded by Queen Cleopatra VII after she formed a pact with the Herald.
*   **Mission:** To protect the Herald’s tomb and ensure his safe awakening.
*   **Modern State (2070):** They live in a hidden desert settlement. While they maintain a small paramilitary force, their resources are dwindling compared to global corporations.
*   **The Secret:** Not all members of the settlement know the truth. Most believe they are preserving a sacred religious tradition, while only the "chosen ones" (the leadership) understand the alien nature of their charge.

## 2. The Secret Organization
A high-tech, paramilitary corporation that hunts alien artifacts and "wetware."

*   **Origins:** Traces back to power-hungry factions in Ancient Egypt who saw the Heralds not as gods, but as sources of power to be seized.
*   **Resources:** In 2070, they possess immense wealth and advanced silicon-based technology, including combat robots and sophisticated sensors.
*   **Goal:** To capture a Herald (alive or dead) to reverse-engineer their biotechnology and achieve global dominance.
*   **Leadership:** Commanded by field officers who are as ruthless as they are efficient, viewing the Followers of Cleopatra as mere obstacles to a "jackpot."

## 3. The Hidden Monster Society (The Hybrids)
Descendants of the biological constructs (monsters) created by the Heralds in ancient times.

*   **Nature:** These are biological hybrids (e.g., Minotaurs, Werewolves) built on a human genetic template.
*   **Status:** They live secretly among the Followers of Cleopatra and in the shadows of the world.
*   **Compatibility:** They are genetically compatible with humans and can interbreed, though such occurrences are rare.
*   **Defense:** They utilize advanced memory-erasure technology to ensure that any outsider who sees them forgets their existence.
