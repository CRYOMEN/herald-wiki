# History of the Heralds and Earth

## 1. The Ancient Origin
The story begins in a distant galaxy within a binary planet system. This system was home to an advanced alien civilization whose technology was based on bioengineering ("wetware") rather than silicon. Their world began to die, consumed by an encroaching "Cosmic Threat"—an extinction-level event that remains the ultimate antagonist of the series.

### The Sister Planet Theory
Alien scientists discovered that their planets possessed a unique resonance with a third world: Earth. This biological and cosmic connection meant that Earth was a "sister planet," sharing a compatible evolutionary path. Heralds were sent to Earth to warn humanity and accelerate their development so they could eventually help resist the Cosmic Threat.

## 2. The Dawn of Egypt (c. 3200 – 3000 BC)
While several Heralds were sent to different regions (explaining global myths of dragons and hybrids), the protagonist, **Alekandras**, arrived in the Nile Valley. Young and impulsive, he was not the intended Herald for this mission but had "stolen" the role out of a naive desire to save both worlds.

Upon arrival, he found humanity in a primitive state, lacking writing, music, or centralized government. He spent the next three millennia secretly influencing the development of Egyptian culture.
*   **The Institution of Pharaohs:** Alekandras "pulled the strings" from the shadows to create the first centralized state and the institution of the Pharaohs to organize humanity.
*   **The Age of Pyramids (2700 – 2500 BC):** He provided the knowledge and cultural foundations that led to the construction of the Great Pyramids.
*   **The Middle and New Kingdoms:** The flourishing of these eras was a direct result of the alien technology and culture he introduced.

### The Unified God: Amun-Ra
The people of Egypt initially worshiped **Ra**, the sun god. Alekandras, who could fly and often blocked the sun with his massive form, appeared like a solar deity. However, because he remained hidden to maintain the mission's secrecy, the people began to call him **Amun** (the "Hidden God," the invisible source of existence). Over time, these two concepts were merged into **Amun-Ra**, the "Hidden Sun."

## 3. Notable Dynastic Relationships
Alekandras formed profound connections with several influential figures throughout history:
*   **Hatshepsut (c. 15th Century BC):** A female Pharaoh of the 18th Dynasty. She bore the title "Wife of the God Amun" and was a literal partner to Alekandras. He used his power to help her ascend the throne, and she dedicated her mortuary temple at Deir el-Bahari to him.
*   **Cleopatra VII and Iras (c. 1st Century BC):** During Cleopatra's reign, Alekandras fell in love with **Iras**, a blonde woman of Scandinavian origin who had been given to him as a "sacrifice." Iras became the mediator between the Herald and the royal court.

### The Biomech Civil War
A rival Herald named **Apophis** went rogue, seeking to sabotage the mission. This led to a "biomech civil war" where Alekandras created armies of biological hybrids (the Bestiary) to defend humanity. Though victorious, the conflict severely depleted his Living Suit's "special energy."

## 4. Hibernation (c. 30 BC)
By the end of Cleopatra's reign, Alekandras entered deep hibernation within a hidden desert tomb. He calculated that humanity would reach a technological "silicon age" by the year 2200 AD. He entrusted his safety to the **Followers of Cleopatra**, a group of loyal subjects sworn to guard him for eternity.

## 5. The Awakening (2070 AD)
In the year 2070, a **Secret Organization** located the tomb. During their assault, the awakening process was sabotaged, causing a massive thermal energy drain that froze the region. A teleportation anomaly occurred, sending Alekandras to a modern metropolis 130 years earlier than planned.
