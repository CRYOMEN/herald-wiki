# The Homeworld

The Homeworld is the primary origin of the Herald species and the birthplace of the bio-organic technology ("wetware") that has shaped human mythology.

## 1. Celestial Profile
*   **Location:** Located in a distant galaxy, far beyond the reach of human astronomical charts.
*   **System:** A binary planet system orbiting a single star. The two worlds exist in a "gravitational dance," locked in a close orbit that has fostered a unique and interconnected biosphere.
*   **Nature:** The Homeworld was a thriving, bio-diverse environment where technology and nature were indistinguishable.

## 2. The Sister Planet Theory
The Heralds did not discover Earth by chance. Their scientists identified a unique **Planetary Resonance**—a shared vibrational and biological frequency that Earth emits, mirroring that of their own worlds.
*   **Evolutionary Convergence:** Because of this resonance, life on both the Homeworld and Earth shares a compatible evolutionary path. This "Sisterhood" ensures that alien biology and biotechnology are fundamentally compatible with terrestrial life.
*   **The Singing World:** Earth was identified as a "Sister Planet" that was "singing" on the same frequency as the Homeworld, making it the only viable destination for their mission of survival.

## 3. The Cosmic Threat
The Homeworld is currently in a state of terminal decline. It is being consumed by an extinction-level event known simply as the **Cosmic Threat**.
*   **Nature:** The exact nature of the threat is unknown—it may be a predatory species, a virulent pathogen, or a dark matter phenomenon.
*   **Impact:** The threat consumes entire planetary systems. Having already snuffed out the life of the Homeworld binary system, it is now expanding across the void toward Earth.
*   **The Mission:** The Herald mission was launched not for conquest, but as a desperate warning. The goal is to prepare Earth’s civilizations for the arrival of this force before both worlds are lost.

## 4. Society and Governance
*   **The Twin Planet Council:** The Homeworld was governed by a central authority that oversaw the selection of Heralds for the Earth mission.
*   **Political Factions:** The Heralds were not a monolith. Their society was marked by internal disagreements regarding the Earth mission. While many believed in guiding humanity as mentors, others (such as the rogue Herald **Apophis**) viewed humanity with contempt or sought to exploit the Sister Planet for their own ends.

## 5. The Heralds’ Legacy
Multiple Heralds were dispatched to Earth, landing in diverse regions such as the Nile Valley, Northern Europe, Greece, and Asia. Each Herald brought their own Living Suit configuration and regional biological templates, leading to the localized mythologies of hybrids and monsters that define human history.
