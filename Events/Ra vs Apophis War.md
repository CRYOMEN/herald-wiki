# Event: War with Apophis

## Overview

A biomech civil war fought in the Nile Valley between [Alekandras](../Characters/Alekandras.md) (Amun-Ra) and [Apophis](../Characters/Apophis.md) — two heralds from the same [Homeworld](../Locations/Homeworld.md) whose missions diverged into open conflict. The war is the real-world basis for the Egyptian mythological conflict between Ra (the sun god) and Apophis (the great serpent, embodiment of chaos).

## Date

TBD. Took place during Alekandras' time in Ancient Egypt (~3000–1070 BC), but precise dating is unresolved.

## Cause

- The heralds had internal politics and disagreements.
- Apophis was corrupted by (or aligned with) the cosmic threat, apparently to sabotage the heralds' mission on Earth.
- While Alekandras worked to guide humanity toward a civilization capable of resisting the cosmic threat, Apophis turned hostile and sought to destroy everything Alekandras was building.

## Combatants

- **Alekandras** (Amun-Ra) and his armies of bio-engineered creatures.
- **Apophis** and his armies of serpent monsters and other beasts — created using his own Living Suit to destroy Alekandras' work.

## Outcome

- **Alekandras won** a partial victory: he drove Apophis into hiding, and Apophis survived.
- **Apophis's current status is unknown** (hiding, hibernating, or dead — TBD).
- His serpent creatures and other beast creations may still exist.
- The war **drained Alekandras' special energy to critically low levels** — a contributing factor in his eventual decision to enter hibernation.

## Mythological Legacy

The conflict became the source of the Egyptian myth of **Ra vs. Apophis**: the nightly battle of the sun god against the great serpent of chaos. The human interpretation cast the real biomech war as a cosmic, eternal struggle between light and darkness.
