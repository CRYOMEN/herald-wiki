# Character Sheet - Iras

## 1. Identity
- **Name:** Iras (Real name unknown).
- **Alias(es):** The Messenger; Mediator of the Gods; The Rainbow (Iris).
- **Type:** Human (Scandinavian).
- **Role:** Supporting Historical Character; Ancestor of Jasmine.

## 2. Physical
- **Description:** Exceptionally beautiful with long blonde hair, refined features, and pale skin. Her appearance was considered exotic and "like a star in the black sky" to the people of Egypt.

## 3. Backstory
- **Origins:** Originally from a Scandinavian region, she was captured at age 10 and sold into slavery. After training in court etiquette in Greece, she was resold to the court of Cleopatra VII.
- **The Sacrifice:** Cleopatra presented Iras to **Alekandras** (Amun-Ra) as a high-value sacrifice. Instead of consuming her, Alekandras was captivated by her and the two fell in love.
- **The Mediator:** She served as the primary link between the Herald and Cleopatra’s court, earning her the name "Iras" (after the Greek goddess Iris, the messenger).
- **The DNA Legacy:** When Alekandras entered hibernation in 30 BC, Iras remained behind. Fearing that their connection would be lost to time, she asked the assistant **Iko** to modify her DNA. This modification encoded her memories and feelings, ensuring they would be passed down to her descendants as "faint echoes."

## 4. Relationships
- **Alekandras:** Her lover and "God." She was the only person who truly understood the bridge between his alien nature and his human emotions.
- **Cleopatra VII:** Her queen and friend.
- **Iko:** Her co-conspirator in the DNA modification project.
- **Jasmine:** Her descendant in 2070.

## 5. The Genetic Device
Before her death, Iko provided Iras (or her lineage) with a special device tuned to her modified DNA. This device can amplify the "faint echoes" of her memories into vivid, lived experiences for her descendants.
