# Character Sheet - Cleopatra VII

## 1. Identity
- **Name:** Cleopatra VII Philopator.
- **Type:** Human.
- **Role:** Queen of Egypt; Founder of the Followers; Strategic Ally of Alekandras.

## 2. Psychological / Temperament
- **Personality:** Brilliant, pragmatic, and visionary. She was one of the few humans capable of looking past the "divinity" of the Heralds to understand their scientific mission.
- **Drives:** To ensure the survival of Egypt and the success of Alekandras’ mission. She viewed the Herald not just as a god, but as a peer and a friend.

## 3. Backstory
- **The Pact:** During her reign (51–30 BC), Cleopatra formed a secret alliance with Alekandras. She provided him with political protection and resources in exchange for his guidance.
- **The Sacrifice of Iras:** Understanding Alekandras' need for connection and "neuro-resonance," she presented her servant **Iras** to him. This act was both a diplomatic gesture and a personal one, as Iras became the Herald's most trusted companion.
- **The Sacred Oath:** As the Roman conquest of Egypt became inevitable and Alekandras prepared for hibernation, Cleopatra issued a final, secret command to her most loyal subjects: they were to guard the Herald’s tomb for eternity. This oath founded the **Followers of Cleopatra**, the community that would protect the Herald for the next 2,000 years.

## 4. Relationships
- **Alekandras:** Her mentor and "God." Their relationship was built on mutual respect and a shared concern for the future of Earth.
- **Iras:** Her handmaiden and the woman she entrusted with the Herald’s heart.
- **The Followers:** The lineage of guardians who view her command as their ultimate purpose.

## 5. Significance
Cleopatra is the architect of the Herald's survival. Without her foresight and the loyalty she inspired, Alekandras would have likely been discovered or destroyed by power-hungry factions long before 2070.
