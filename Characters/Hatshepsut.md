# Character Sheet - Hatshepsut

## 1. Identity
- **Name:** Hatshepsut (Maat-Ka-Ra).
- **Type:** Human.
- **Role:** Pharaoh of the 18th Dynasty; Early Ally and Partner of Alekandras.

## 2. Psychological / Temperament
- **Personality:** Ambitious, pious, and highly intelligent. She was one of the first human leaders to successfully harness the "divine" aura of a Herald to consolidate power and bring peace to Egypt.
- **Drives:** To stabilize the New Kingdom and legitimize her rule through her unique connection to the "Hidden God," Amun-Ra.

## 3. Backstory
- **Ascension:** Daughter of Thutmose I, Hatshepsut ascended to the throne around 1478 BC. Her reign was marked by unprecedented prosperity and the construction of architectural marvels.
- **The Divine Alliance:** Hatshepsut claimed to be the literal daughter of Amun-Ra. In reality, she was one of the first humans to form an intimate, symbiotic partnership with **Alekandras**. He provided her with the technological and cultural "miracles" needed to legitimize her rule, while her devotion provided him with a vital source of neuro-resonance energy.
- **Wife of the God Amun:** She held the highest religious title of the priesthood, serving as the mediator between the Herald and the people of Thebes. Her mortuary temple at **Deir el-Bahari** remains a testament to their collaboration.

## 4. Relationships
- **Alekandras:** Her partner and "divine father." Their union was the foundation of the 18th Dynasty's spiritual and political power.
- **The Theban Priesthood:** She used her connection to Alekandras to bring the priesthood under her direct influence.

## 5. Significance
Hatshepsut represents the "Golden Age" of the Herald’s time on Earth. Under her rule, the cooperation between human statehood and alien technology reached its zenith, proving that the two species could thrive together.
