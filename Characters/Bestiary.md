# Bestiary

The Bestiary is a catalog of the biological hybrids ("monsters") created by the Heralds using their Living Suits. These creatures served as guardians, laborers, and soldiers throughout human history, and their presence gave rise to the world's many mythologies.

## 1. Creation Mechanics
The Living Suit acts as a mobile genetic laboratory. By sampling local DNA and mixing it with the Herald's own bio-templates, the suit can synthesize entirely new species.
- **Energy Cost:** Creation consumes the suit's "Special Energy."
- **Decomposition:** Upon death, hybrids decompose rapidly into base biomass proteins. This "self-cleaning" mechanism prevents any fossil evidence of their existence from being discovered by modern science.

## 2. Biological Traits
- **Sentience:** Most hybrids are sentient and capable of speech and complex emotion.
- **Physicality:** They are typically much larger and stronger than humans (averaging 2.4 meters in height), with a mix of human and animal characteristics (claws, fangs, fur, or scales).
- **Behavior:** Their temperaments are "wild" and heavily influenced by their animal DNA.
- **Reproduction:** Hybrids are genetically compatible with humans due to the human DNA used in their creation. While they cannot reproduce with each other effectively, they can produce offspring through unions with humans. The resulting children are always of the hybrid type and grow at an accelerated rate.
- **Drives:** Many hybrids possess a high libido and a natural attraction to humans, leading to the many "romances" between gods, monsters, and mortals found in ancient myths.

## 3. Secrecy and Survival
In 2070, a small population of hybrids still exists, living in secret within the Follower settlements or in the shadows of major cities.
- **Memory Erasure:** Hybrid societies utilize advanced memory-modification technology to ensure that any human who encounters them—or any hybrid who leaves the community—has their memories altered to protect their secret.

## 4. Notable Species

### Canines
- **Anubis:** A jackal-headed humanoid created by Alekandras in Ancient Egypt. Worshipped as a god of the dead.
- **Werewolves:** Human-wolf hybrids found in European and North American folklore.

### Felines
- **Bastet:** Cat-headed humanoids of Egypt.
- **Sphinx:** Lion-human hybrids of Egypt and Greece.

### Ungulates
- **Minotaur:** Bull-headed humanoids of the Mediterranean.
- **Satyrs:** Goat-human hybrids.

### Reptiles
- **Lizardmen:** Human-lizard hybrids. One of the most common species created by Alekandras, frequently appearing in the modern narrative.
- **Sobek:** Crocodile-headed humanoids of the Nile.

### Others
- **Centaurs:** Human-horse hybrids.
- **Unicorns:** Fully zoomorphic sentient creatures created by other Heralds (e.g., Arturas in England).
- **Harpy/Angel:** Human-bird hybrids.
