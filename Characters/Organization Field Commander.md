# Character Sheet - Organization Field Commander

## 1. Identity
- **Name:** Unknown (TBD).
- **Alias(es):** The Commander; The Asset Recovery Officer.
- **Type:** Human.
- **Role:** Primary Antagonist (Prologue).

## 2. Psychological / Temperament
- **Personality:** Ruthless, efficient, and deeply pragmatic. He possesses the cold, detached professionalism of a high-level corporate operative. He views the Heralds not as gods or even as people, but as "high-value assets" to be captured and exploited.
- **Drives:** To secure alien "wetware" for the Secret Organization and advance his own standing within the corporation.
- **Inspiration:** Reminiscent of characters like Colonel Miles Quaritch—a man who believes that superior firepower and modern tactics can overcome any "mythological" threat.

## 3. Backstory
- **Origins:** A veteran of corporate wars and high-stakes asset recovery missions. He was specifically chosen for the Egypt operation due to his lack of superstition and his tactical brilliance.
- **The Siege of the Settlement:** He commanded the assault on the Follower settlement. He viewed the defenders as "primitive cultists" and was caught off guard by the physics of the "freezing vortex" triggered by the Herald's awakening.
- **Survival:** He survived the vortex by retreating into his command vehicle's reinforced, temperature-controlled interior. He now views the Herald as a personal challenge—a "white whale" he must hunt down in the metropolis.

## 4. Relationships
- **The Secret Organization:** His employers. They provide him with near-unlimited resources but expect results.
- **The Settlement Leader:** His primary obstacle and personal rival during the prologue.
- **Alekandras:** His "Target." He is one of the few humans who understands the physical reality of the Living Suits without being blinded by religious awe.

## 5. Abilities & Gear
- **Tactical Command:** An expert in combined-arms warfare, utilizing tanks, drones, and high-tech infantry.
- **Corporate Tech:** Equipped with the best silicon-based technology 2070 has to offer, including thermal scanners, signal dampeners, and heavy-duty exoskeletons.

## 6. Significance
The Commander represents the pinnacle of human "hardware" and military logic. He serves as a direct foil to Alekandras’ "wetware" and idealistic mission, representing a humanity that has grown powerful enough to try and "dissect" its former gods.
